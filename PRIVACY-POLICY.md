Brock ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to not exploiting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the Brock browser extension (the "Extension").

1. Information Collection and Storage
Brock operates on a strict 100% offline, zero-tracking architecture.

No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any personally identifiable information, browsing history, user telemetry, or analytics.

Local Storage Only: All extension settings, block lists, timer configurations, and preferences are stored exclusively on your local device using the browser's native chrome.storage API. This data never leaves your computer and is never transmitted to any external server.

2. Permissions and How They Are Used

Our extension requests only the minimum necessary permissions required for its core functionality:

storage: Used locally to save your Focus timer intervals and blocked website preferences on your device.

alarms: Used locally to run the Focus timer and break countdown timers in the background.

declarativeNetRequest & host_permissions (<all_urls>): Used strictly on your local machine to match and intercept traffic against your user-defined blocked website rules without logging or inspecting your browsing activity.

3. Third-Party Sharing
   
Because Brock does not collect any user data or personal information, we do not share, sell, rent, or trade any information with third parties. There are no external analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or cloud databases integrated into the extension.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional updates or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated effective date.
            
5. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy or Brock's data practices, you can reach out via GitHub or through our developer store listing page.
